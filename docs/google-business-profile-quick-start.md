**Fix**: Complete verification, wait 48 hours

**Problem**: Can't upload photos
**Fix**: Use Google Business Profile mobile app

**Problem**: Low profile views
**Fix**: Add more photos, post regularly, optimize description

**Problem**: Negative review
**Fix**: Respond professionally, offer to resolve offline
